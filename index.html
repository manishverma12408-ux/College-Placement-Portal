About the Portal

The College Placement Portal helps students find placement opportunities, allows companies to post job openings, and enables placement officers to manage the complete recruitment process.

Portal Features

Student

  • Create Profile
  • View Job Opportunities
  • Apply for Companies
  • Track Applications
  • View Placement Status

Company

  • Company Registration
  • Post Job Opportunities
  • View Eligible Students
  • Shortlist Candidates
  • Update Selection Status

Placement Officer

  • Manage Students
  • Manage Companies
  • Approve Job Posts
  • Manage Placement Drives
  • Generate Reports
